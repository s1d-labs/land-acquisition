INTORDUCTION:

These proceedings are under provision of Section 23 of the Right to Fair Compensation
and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013 for determination
_of compensation in respect of net area of land proposed to be acquired rmneasuring 64 Kanai 03
Marla for the purpose of construction of construction of 24 Mtr Span Bridge at Km 32.900
(Marpoyul Sapi) and Construction of 40 Mtr Span Bridge at KM 29.580 (Sethchen Sapi) on Sankoo-
Kanoor-Sapila-Mulbekh Road. The land acquisition proceeding were initiated with the publication
of preliminary Notification under section 11 of the Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in
Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013.

The Officer Commanding, 55 RCC (GREF), C/o 56 APO had submitted a land acquisition
proposal of construction of 24 Mtr Span Bridge at Km 32.900 (Marpoyul Sapi) and Construction of
40 Mtr Span Bridge at KM 29.580 (Sethchen Sapi) on Sankoo-Kanoor-Sapila-Mulbekh Road.

2. PUBLICATION OF NOTIFICATION AND ISSUE OF NOTICES:

The local SIA team was notified as Social Assessment Team SIA Team U/S 4 of the Right
to Fair Compensation and Transparency in the land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement
Act, 2913 for conducting Social impact Assessment (SAI) study vide No. DC-K/LAC-RTFCR-
LARR/2021 dated: 22.09.2021. SAI Team submitted a draft SAI report and public hearing were
conducted. Final SIA report was submitted to the multi-Disciplinary Expert Group for appraisal
constituted. The Multi-Disciplinary Expert Group submitted its Recommendation.

The SIA report and the recommendation of Multi-Disciplinary Expert Group was then
approved by the Appropriate Government i.e. Deputy Commissioner, UT, Ladakh and a preliminary
Notification No. DC-K/LAC-Bridges/GREF/2021/61 dated: 04.12.2021 was issued u/s 11 of the Act
stating that the land described in the schedule there to (there after referred to as the said
land/land) was needed or likely to be needed for the public purpose. The notification was issued
for inviting objections within 60 days, authorizing survey and settlement officer and his staff for
conducting survey u/s 12 of the Act. The Addl. Deputy Commissioner, Kargil was appointed as

7. LOCATION OF THE LAND.

The total acquired land admeasuring 04 Kanal 03 Marla is situated in Sapi Village of Kargil
District.

8. VALUATION OF THE LAND.

The principal for assessing the amount of compensation to be awarded has been laid
down under Section 26 of the Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition,
Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013 and accordingly the market value of the land was
determined for further determination of Compensation.

9. DETERMINATION OF COMPENSATION.

As per the provision of Section 30 of the Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in
Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013, 100% of the compensation amount
(includes market value of land multiplies by multiplication factor) is to be awarded as solatium as
has been determined in the Award statement.

10. COMPENSATION OF LAND.

The compensation of land is to be paid to land interested persons. The total amount of
award workout to Rs. 53,38,602.00 (Rupees Fifty Three Lakh Thirty eight thousand Six
hundred Two only).
