5. Market value:-

The Tahsildar, Kalla has obtained registration sale transactions on the sales taken place for
a period of 3 years preceding the date of publication of Preliminary Notification.

As seen from the Registration Statistics for the preceding 3 years from the date of
publication of P.N., there are altogether 3 sale transactions reportedly held during the three years
crucial period (i.e.,) 2 sales in 2013 and 1 sales in 2014 and no sales in 2012 & 2015 in the
relevant period. Only sale deeds have been taken for fixation of market value.

According to Section 26 of Right to Fair compensation and Transparency in Land
Acquisition, Rehabilitation & Resettlement Act,2013 (No.30 of 2013) which came in to force w.e.f
1.1.2014 following is the criteria in assessing and determining the market value of the lands
namely:-

a. the market value, if any, specified in the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 for the
registration of sale deeds or agreements to sell, as the case may be, in the area,
where the land is situated; or

b. the average sale price for similar type of land situated in the nearest village or
nearest vicinity area; or

Page 4 of 8
Cc. Consented amount of compensation as agreed upon under sub- section (2) of
section 2 in case of acquisition of lands for private companies or for public
private partnership projects; whichever is higher.

The land under proposal is adjacent to the existing Railway line
separated by around 30 metres from the track. All the lands in R.S.Nos.
138,578,139 & 141/1 are in a closed pack and R.S.No. 581/2 & 581/3 are a
Linear strict very close and parallel to the track separated by 250 mts., from the
land proposed for substation. All the lands except the later have standing wet
paddy surrounded by other paddy lands. All these lands are lying close and
parallel to a R&B road. The said lands doesn’t have any trees/ permanent
structures/ tombs/ burial yards etc., and are suitable for the proposed activity.

As referred from Sec. 26 of LA Act - 2013, criteria (a) states that the
market value, if any specified in the Stamp Act 1899, for registration of sale deeds
or agreements to sell, as the case may be, in the area, where the land ts situated:
This clearly signifies the market value of any land in the area in which land to be
acquired is also available. The R.S.Nos. 138/2, 581/2& 581/3 are the lands
which carry basic value of Rs. 50,00,000/- as indicated in criteria (a) which is the
highest possible among the R.S.Nos. 578/1, 139 & 141/2 whose values are Rs.
15 Lakhs, Rs. 35 Lakhs & Rs. 35 Lakhs respectively.

Criteria (b) states that the average sale for similar type of land situated in
the nearest village or nearest vicinity area ; or —- which comes to Rs. 48,40,000/-
whichever is higher — then, the rate of Rs. 50,00,000/- calculated from criteria (a)
shall be adopted for valuation of the entire stretch of land as all this R.S.Nos. are
in a closed pack of Ac. 1.17 cts., except for R.S.Nos. 581/2 &581/3 which has
the market value of Rs. 50 Lakhs.

Accordingly the basic value of Rs. 50,00,000/- to the lands covered by
R.S.No. 138/2 etc., can be considered as market value for the present
Acquisition, which is the higher value when compared with (a) & (b) categories.
The (c) category is not applicable in this LA matter.

In view of the above, I have fixed the land value for the lands under
acquisition covered by R.S.Nos. 138/2 etc., of Pedamiram Village is
Rs.50,00,000/- per acre excluding statutory benefits.

There are no Trees or Structures involved in the land covered by acquisition.
