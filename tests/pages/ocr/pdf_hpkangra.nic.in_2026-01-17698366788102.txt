1. The Department of Tourism and Civil Aviation, Government of HP proposed
acquisition of land belonging to private owners of 14 villages (10 from Tehsil

Airport.” Accordingly, the land acquisition process has been initiated under
“The Right to Fair Compensation & Transparency in Land Acquisition
Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act 2013” (Hereinafter referred to as
RFCTLARR Act 2013).
