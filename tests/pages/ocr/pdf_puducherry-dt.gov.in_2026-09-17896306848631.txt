SUMMARY OF LAND ACQUISITION PROCEEDINGS UNDER
SECTION 37 (3) OF THE RFCTLARR ACT, 2013 IN RESPECT OF
REVISED SUPPLEMENTARY AWARD No.1/2026, DATED: 03.09.2026
L. WHEREAS, at the instance of the Director, Adi Dravidar Welfare
Department, Puducherry, land acquisition proceedings under the Land
Acquisition Act, 1894 were initiated for acquisition of land measuring
0.46.50 H.A.Ca comprised in R.S. No.5/5 of Suthukeni Revenue Village, for the
public purpose of providing free house sites to SC/OEBC beneficiaries.
Notification under Section 4(1) was issued vide G.O.Ms.No.51 dated
07.06.2007, Declaration under Section 6 was issued vide G.O.Ms.No.29 dated
18.08.2008, and Award No.5/2010 was passed on 16.04.2010 for an amount of
Rs.6,85,426.00.
2. AND WHEREAS, the said amount could not be disbursed owing to non-
production of title documents by the landowners and the pendency of a
reference sought by Thiru. Karthikeyan under Section 18 of the 1894 Act. In
compliance with Section 31(2) of the said Act, the awarded amount was
deposited before the competent Civil Court on 29.01.2015, along with a
reference under Section 30 seeking adjudication on title and apportionment.
a AND WHEREAS, the landowner, Thiru S. Karthikeyan, had earlier filed
W.P. No.11117/2010 seeking to restrain the acquisition proceedings. An interim
injunction restraining dispossession was granted on 17.06.2010. The said writ
petition was disposed of on 28.02.2014 directing appointment of an independent

surveyor. Consequent thereto, possession of the acquired land was taken on
19.05.2014.
4. AND WHEREAS, thereafter, the petitioner filed W.P. No.6729/2014 and
subsequently W.P. No.6964/2015 seeking compensation under the RFCTLARR
Act, 2013. By order dated 12.06.2017, the Hon'ble High Court directed fixation
of compensation under the 2013 Act. The Writ Appeal preferred thereagainst in
W.A. No.524/2018 was dismissed on 09.01.2024, holding that both possession
(19.05.2014) and deposit (January 2015) occurred after the commencement of
the 2013 Act.
5. .AND WHEREAS, the Government, having regard to the protracted
nature of the acquisition spanning fifteen years and the paramount public
interest, directed vide I.D. Notes dated 16.06.2025 that compensation shall be
disbursed under the provisions of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013.
6. AND WHEREAS, it is hereby recorded that the directions of the Hon'ble
High Court are confined solely to re-determination of compensation. The
acquisition proceedings under the 1894 Act have neither been quashed nor
declared to have lapsed, and all notifications, declarations and actions taken
thereunder remain valid and operative.
7. AND WHEREAS, pursuant to the said orders, compensation was re-
determined under the RFCTLARR Act, 2013 by adopting the date of Section
4(1) notification (07.06.2007) as the reference date, in accordance with Rule
34(i) of the Puducherry RFCTLARR Rules, 2016 (as amended in 2017), and
Supplementary Award No.1/2026 dated 21.04.2026 was passed.

8. AND WHEREAS, subsequently, in a connected land acquisition case at
Karaikal involving one Tmt. Balkees Ummal, the validity of Rule 34(i) of the
Puducherry RFCTLARR Rules, 2016 came up for consideration before the
Hon'ble High Court in W.P. No.12980/2020. By order dated 02.03.2021, the
Hon'ble High Court struck down the 2017 Amendment and further held that
even the original Rule 34(i) as it stood cannot be sustained.
9. AND WHEREAS, aggrieved thereby, the Government of Puducherry
preferred SLP (C) No.006288 of 2023 before the Hon'ble Supreme Court, which
was dismissed on 23.03.2026. The Review Petition filed thereagainst in Diary
No.35305/2026 was also dismissed on 21.07.2026. Thus, the striking down of
Rule 34(i) has attained finality.
10. AND WHEREAS, it is pertinent to note that the earlier Supplementary
Award (Supplementary Award No.1/2026 dated 21.04.2026) was expressly
made subject to the condition that the valuation adopted therein
was provisional and contingent upon the final outcome of the litigation
concerning Rule 34(i). In view of the dismissal of the SLP and the Review
Petition, the provisional valuation has ceased to be operative.
11. AND WHEREAS, consequently, the office of the Sub-Collector
(Revenue) — cum — Land Acquisition Officer, Karaikal, referred the matter to
the Law Department, Government of Puducherry, vide e-office File No.
M/53185/2026 - O/o the Tahsildar (Dist. Magt) - KKL, through the District
Collector — cum — Special Secretary (Revenue) and the Development
Commissioner — cum — Secretary (Revenue), seeking clarification on the
following issues:

Page 3 of 11

(i) | The appropriate reference date to be adopted for determination of
market value in cases falling under Section 24(1)(a) of the
RFCTLARR Act, 2013;

(ii) Whether the benefit extended in the present case may also be
extended to other similar land acquisition projects;

(iii) The appropriate date for calculation of Additional Market Value at
the rate of 12% per annum; and

(iv) The uniform procedure to be followed in dealing with cases falling
under Section 24(1)(a) of the Act.

12. AND WHEREAS, upon consideration of the Order in W.P.
No.12980/2020, the Division Bench Judgment in Sri Venkateswara Educational
and Charitable Trust Vs. The Secretary to Government of Tamil Nadu and Ors.
(W.A. Nos.1063 of 2012 etc., batch dated 17.10.2022), and the Clarificatory
Note dated 26.10.2015 of the Government of India, the Law Department
rendered its considered opinion, vide Note # 16, 17 & 18 (Para Nos.37 to 48)
dated 25.08.2026 of the above said file, holding that:

(i) 01.01.2014 shall be taken as the base date for determination of
market value for cases falling under Section 24(1)(a) of the Act;

(ii) The said legal principle shall apply to all similarly situated land
acquisition projects;

(iii) 01.01.2014 is the appropriate date from which additional market
value is to be calculated; and

(iv) The Revenue Department shall devise a uniform procedure for
dealing with cases under Section 24(1)(a) of the Act.

13. AND WHEREAS, the Law Department further opined that the Division
Bench Judgment in Sri Venkateswara Educational and Charitable Trust is
holding the field and is binding on this Administration, as the Madras High
Court is the jurisdictional High Court for the Union Territory of Puducherry
under Section 9 of the Puducherry (Administration) Act, 1963. Even though the
Administration is not a party to the said Judgment, the legal principles
enunciated therein are binding precedents for similar land acquisition
proceedings in the Union Territory of Puducherry.

14. AND WHEREAS, in view of the finality attained in the litigation
concerning Rule 34(i), the binding judicial pronouncements, and the opinion of
the Law Department, the reference date for determination of market value in the
present case is hereby adopted as 01.01.2014, being the date of commencement
of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013. This adoption is in strict compliance with the
statutory mandate, the binding judicial precedents, and the opinion of the Law
Department.
